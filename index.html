How I lead

What my teams say

I lead by connecting people, not by standing above the work. My teams have grown, my stakeholders have been brought into the research early, and I still pick up the fieldwork myself when the project needs it.

The four habits below are how I run a team. The quotes come from people who reported to me or worked beside me.

01

I grow the people around me

I treat development as part of the job: sharing what I know, creating room to try things, and handing over ownership. At Dunia Games the goal was a team that could run UX research independently.

“Her willingness to share knowledge, and her incredible ability to uplift and empower others.”Kadek Nanda PuspasariFormer direct report
02

I make people feel safe to do good work

A team does its best thinking when everyone is comfortable speaking up. I pay attention to each person, and I act as a mentor before I act as a reviewer.

“Always finds a way to make everyone feel comfortable at work.”Radhia AlyaniFormer direct report
03

I connect the functions that need each other

Most of the people I align are stakeholders, not direct reports. On Buyer Journey I introduced teams to each other before the research began, then shared small insights along the way so interest stayed alive.

“A natural ability to align diverse perspectives.”Joelia Fitri Adhistiscia, Head of Consumer Insights & Monitoring, Bank Danamon
04

I keep the standard high and the method shared

I like frameworks the whole team can use, such as the standardised user testing framework the SEA research team adopted, and the panel playbook and research portal at Tokopedia. Rigour should not depend on one person.

“A very critical and detailed researcher.”Benny Alexandra, AVP of Product Design, Astro
